    Vector

    Real-Time Hand Gesture-Controlled 3D Particle Manipulation System: glowing anatomical models (brain, beating heart, lungs, skeleton, organs), scientific wonders, DNA, and engines made of over 100,000 GPU particles — manipulated in real time with your hand via camera tracking.

    1. Snap your fingers to summon particles.
    2. Make a fist to assemble the wonder, organ or machine.
    3. Open your hand: wonders morph to the next one — organs, engines and voxels explode to show every part inside, named with what it does. Close your hand to put it back together.
    4. Twist your hand to turn the model, raise or lower it to tilt; two hands zoom.
    5. Point ☝ at a part to learn what it does, pinch 🤏 to pull it out. Try the 🎓 quiz, 🎤 voice, ✂ cut-away and ⏺ recording.
    6. Show ✌ to jump to the next model.

    ℹ️ If your browser doesn't prompt for camera access, click the lock / camera icon 🔒 next to localhost:5173 in your address bar and set Camera to Allow.
